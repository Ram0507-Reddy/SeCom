01 // Ephemeral Secure Chat Guide

Follow these steps to establish a secure, self-destructing end-to-end encrypted session.

  1. Authenticate Identity: Enter any handle or username to start. The application instantly generates a local, browser-based 256-bit ECDH key pair in-memory.
  2. Generate or Join Channel: Click "Generate New Channel" to create a randomized 6-digit room, or input an active room ID shared by your peer.
  3. Key Synchronization: Once both peers connect to the channel, their public keys are automatically synced. The application derives a shared secret in RAM.
  4. Secure Chat: Enter your message. It is encrypted locally with AES-GCM-256 before transit. Ciphertext is stored temporarily in Firestore and deleted automatically 15 seconds after decryption.

03 // Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common security, architectural, and operational questions.

How does the 15-second self-destruct mechanism work?

When a recipient decrypts an incoming message, a Firestore timestamp modification updates decryptedAt. The client listens to this update and starts a 15-second countdown. Once expired, the client calls a delete operation on the Firestore document. If a client attempts to bypass this by refreshing, the Firestore security rules reject any attempt to read or re-decrypt, and any subsequent client instantly enforces deletion.

Are messages readable by Google, Firestore, or administrators?

No. All messages are encrypted directly inside your browser's Web Crypto sandbox before being sent to Firestore. The private keys required to decrypt the payloads are generated dynamically and kept solely in the browser's active RAM. Since no private keys or plaintexts are ever sent to the server, nobody—including database administrators—can decrypt your communications.

Do I need a phone number, email, or account to chat?

No. SeCom is built for absolute anonymity. There are no signups, profiles, emails, or phone numbers. Anonymous session credentials are generated transparently in the background via Firebase Anonymous Auth to enforce database access rules without collecting any identifying metadata.

What happens if the other user disconnects or closes the tab?

When a participant closes the tab or clicks "Disconnect", their session keys are deleted from Firestore, and their in-memory keys are wiped. The channel state is automatically reset, and all pending messages in that channel are purged immediately.

04 // Zero-Log & Privacy Policy

Our commitment to absolute privacy, data minimization, and user security.

Data Minimization Principle: SeCom operates under a strict data minimization model. We do not request, collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). All user-created usernames are arbitrary aliases used purely for UI display.

Message Transit and Storage: Chat payloads are end-to-end encrypted and held in Firestore solely during transit. Message documents are configured with a strict, self-enforced time-to-live (TTL) limit and are deleted permanently 15 seconds after their initial decryption. Deletion is destructive: documents are physically deleted from Google Cloud storage blocks and cannot be recovered.

Crawl and Analytics: Google Analytics is utilized purely to monitor overall page visits and site performance. Analytics data is completely anonymized, does not contain IP addresses, and has zero visibility into room codes, usernames, or message contents.
