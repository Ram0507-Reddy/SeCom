How SeCom Self-Destructing Chat Works

SeCom is a zero-log anonymous chat platform. When you send an encrypted message, it is encrypted in your browser using AES-GCM-256 with keys derived via ECDH P-256 key exchange. The ciphertext is stored temporarily in Firestore and deleted automatically after 15 seconds. No message is ever readable by any server.
