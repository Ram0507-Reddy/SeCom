ZERO-LOG & PRIVACY POLICY

Our operational commitment to user data protection.

1 / Data Minimization

SeCom operates on a strict data minimization protocol. We do not collect, request, parse, or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, or social identifiers. Usernames are temporary, client-side session variables used solely to label text bubbles.

2 / Message Storage & Deletion

All messages are fully encrypted client-side using end-to-end AES-GCM-256 before leaving your browser. Plaintexts are never transmitted over the network or saved in server databases. Ciphertexts are held in Google Cloud Firestore transiently and are permanently physically deleted 15 seconds after recipient decryption. Once deleted, documents are irrecoverable.

3 / Connection Logging

SeCom does not log, track, or record database queries or transit logs. Firebase Hosting serves the static assets. Connection monitoring via Google Analytics tracks raw page visit trends without collecting IP addresses, room numbers, username inputs, or message contents.
